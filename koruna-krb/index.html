Crown & Hearth

Every story begins by a humble hearth. You start as a serf with a small cottage, a couple of fields and a fire to keep alive through the winter. Work, trade and save — buy your freedom, become the village reeve, rise to the nobility and build a keep. One life is one generation: when you die, your heir carries on.

How to play

Life is hard and nothing is handed to you. Build your homestead step by step — every repair and investment counts.

01

Keep the hearth burning

Chop wood, fetch water and keep food in the house. Watch your health, strength, hunger and thirst. When the fire dies in winter, so do you — and at night the wolves come out beyond the village.

02

Work & trade

Sow and harvest your fields, fish, hunt with a licence and sell what you can spare at the market. Take jobs from the reeve or the manor, help your neighbours for a few coins and pay your dues to the lord.

03

Raise a family

Court, marry and raise children. Your kin is your most precious asset: your heir takes over everything you built when your life comes to an end.

04

Rise in rank

Buy your freedom, become reeve and lead the village, earn a title, build a keep, lead a retinue on the lord's campaigns and win a fief — with cottages in other villages too.

PLAY TOGETHER

One village, up to four players.

Start a shared game and send your friends the room code. Everyone plays their own character with their own cottage, while the market, time and villagers are shared. Single-player games are saved in your browser; shared rooms are kept on our server for 10 days after the last visit (Privacy).
