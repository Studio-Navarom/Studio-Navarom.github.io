How to play

Learn the wind, find profitable routes and turn a small sloop into a growing maritime enterprise.

01

Choose your course

Click the sea to set a course or hold and drag to steer. Right-click to lower the sails and stop. Wind affects your speed; sailing into it requires tacking. Reduce sail in storms.

02

Discover & salvage

Click floating cargo or a wreck to sail over and collect it. Explore beyond a region's edge to reveal new waters, islands and ports. Harbor expeditions let you return to regions you have already charted.

03

Trade & upgrade

Click a friendly harbor to approach and dock. Buy local exports and sell where demand is higher: flooding a market lowers prices. Profitable trade improves Merchant Alliance standing. Repair, buy ships and upgrade at equipped ports.

04

Fight & expand

Line up your port or starboard cannons with Q/E, or drop a fire barrel with B. Establish salvage outposts and assign patrols to protect them. Defeat a hostile port's defenses before capturing it.

YOUR ADVENTURE

An ocean worth exploring.

Build outposts where salvage drifts in, upgrade their boats and storage, and choose automatic sales or hauling the goods to market yourself. Merchants, royal patrols and pirates react to your actions. Protect your routes and outposts as you expand into new seas.
