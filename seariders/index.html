How to play

Your first steps, from a small starting ship to a growing empire.

01

Set sail

Begin a new voyage in your sloop. Press W to raise the sails and use A/D to steer. Watch the wind: sailing directly against it slows you down.

02

Haul cargo & salvage

Explore the ocean for floating crates and shipwrecks. Approach slowly and press F to gather cargo or salvage valuable goods.

03

Return to port

Sail to a harbor, approach the dock and press F. Sell cargo in the market, repair your hull and visit the shipyard for ships or upgrades.

04

Fight & expand

Turn your ship to line up broadside cannon fire with Q and E. Build salvage outposts, hire patrol vessels and eventually capture hostile ports.
